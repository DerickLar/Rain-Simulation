## Particle Rain Simulation
![RainFall_Example](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/212f2746-516c-409c-8802-316334f35e8d)<br />
A dynamic particle system to simulate rainfall<br />
The simulation allows for the particles to be affected by variables such as descent speed, wind strength, wind direction<br />

[Simulation Video](https://youtu.be/WJ-OoMPtBls)
## Simulation Controls
All simulation controls are in this control panel:<br />
![SimulationControls](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/fc406903-500f-47ab-a113-ac961a6225b0)<br />
**Descent Speed:** determines the speed of the rain particles<br />
**Toggle on** the wind to have an effect in the rain particles<br />
**Wind Strength:** Determines the affect the wind has on the particles, the higher the stronger<br />
**Wind XY Direction:** Determines the direction on the horizontal plane the wind is coming from<br />
**Wind Z Direction:** Determines the how horizontal or vertical the wind is (Lower = Horizontal, Higher = Vertical) <br />

## Camera Controls
You may use your mouse to control the camera:<br />
- Zoom in and out using scroll wheel<br />
- Hold Mouse 1 to tilt the camera<br />
- Hold Middle Mouse to pan the camera<br />

You may also use these keybinds:<br />
- WASD: To move the camera around<br />
- Q -  Move camera down<br />
- E - Move camera up<br />
- Arrow Keys - To tilt the camera<br />

You may exit the program at any time with **ESC**<br />
